Privacy Policy

Firefly Automation is a personal, non-commercial application used exclusively by its owner.

Google User Data

The application accesses the owner's Gmail account to identify bank statement emails, download relevant attachments, and apply labels to messages that have been successfully processed.

Data Storage and Use

Bank statement information is processed locally and imported into the owner's self-hosted Firefly III installation. The application does not sell user data or share it with advertisers.

Data Retention

Downloaded files and imported financial records are retained according to the owner's local storage and backup configuration. The owner can delete locally stored information and revoke Gmail access through Google Account settings.

Access

The application is not available to external users and does not provide a public registration service.
